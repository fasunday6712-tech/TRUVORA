Home / Trust & Safety
Responsible by design

Trust is earned through transparent, accountable systems.

No badge, review or automated signal should be treated as a guarantee. TRUVORA’s trust framework must protect members while making its limits clear.

Verification

Explain what was checked.

Clearly state what each badge means, who performed the check and when it was completed.

Fair reputation

Evidence over assumptions.

Use relevant evidence, moderation, appeals and safeguards against discriminatory or misleading scoring.

Fraud awareness

Make reporting accessible.

Provide reporting paths, practical safety guidance and proportionate risk alerts.

Privacy & security

Protect information.

Use data minimisation, access controls, secure communication, audit logs and defined retention policies.

Dispute resolution

Give members a fair route.

Create a clear process for raising concerns, reviewing evidence, appealing errors and communicating outcomes.

Transaction safety

Be clear about protections.

Use appropriately licensed payment providers. Do not claim to be a bank, payment institution or escrow provider without authorisation.

Home / Legal & Privacy
Clarity & accountability

Responsible technology begins with clear terms.

This page is a structure for future legal documents, not a substitute for reviewed terms or a privacy policy.

Terms of use

Define eligibility, member responsibilities, permitted use, moderation, dispute processes and service limitations.

Privacy notice

Explain data collected, why it is used, sharing, retention, applicable lawful bases and user rights.

Verification disclosures

State which checks are performed, which providers perform them, what results mean and how errors can be appealed.

Regulatory disclosures

Publish accurate information about payments, escrow and other regulated capabilities only after appropriate review.

Before public launch, have qualified legal and privacy professionals adapt the actual documents to the product, data flows and jurisdictions where TRUVORA operates.
